## 2. Lodging and room configurations for 4 ([Carolina](https://www.pinehurst.com/accommodations/the-carolina-hotel/), [Villas](https://www.pinehurst.com/accommodations/the-carolina-villas/))
- **The Carolina Hotel:** 230 Four-Diamond rooms. Deluxe has 1 king or 2 doubles (250–400 sq ft). **Premium has 2 queens (350–500 sq ft), recommended.** A double-queen suite (500–750 sq ft) is the 5-star-feel upgrade.

- **The Manor:** the most casual of the three. 2-bed rooms. Below Naresh's preference.
- **Carolina Villas:** each villa has 4 keyed bedrooms. A Traditional room has 2 queens (425 sq ft). A Parlor is 750 sq ft with a living area.

## 3. Stay-and-play packages ([B&B Golf](https://www.pinehurst.com/packages/bed-breakfast-golf-package/))
Bed, Breakfast & Golf: lodging, breakfast and 1 round per night per paying adult. Rates are per person per night, before tax and resort fee.
Published stay-and-play rates are per person per night, before tax and the resort fee. They step down sharply in the late-November through February window. In that window the Carolina sits above the Holly Inn and the Manor, and the villas sit higher still. Exact published figures are left out of this copy.
- My mapping of the columns to single/double occupancy is inferred from the page layout. Verify it.
- **Hidden Gems:** 15% below B&B. Covers Nos. 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 (No. 9 newly renovated), cart included, plus 1 Cradle round per stay. It's marketed as a "fall" package; whether it runs on Nov 28 is **UNVERIFIED**.
- Other packages: Premier (all golf, 2-night minimum), Donald Ross (2 nights, 3 rounds, from a few thousand), Thanksgiving Golf Package ([plan page](https://www.pinehurst.com/plan-your-getaway/)).
- Rules: **No. 2, No. 4, No. 10 and No. 11 require a minimum 2-night stay.** No. 2 has a +several hundred premium; Nos. 10 and 11 have +a couple of hundred. Most weekends need 3 nights from mid-March to mid-November, which is why late Nov/Dec works for one night.
- Renu: the package is priced per person, so as the second adult she pays the double-occupancy rate even though she won't use her round. Ask whether a non-golfer rate exists (the "Accommodations & breakfast" package starts at several hundred).

## 4. Golf fees and access ([Golf](https://www.pinehurst.com/golf/), [FAQ](https://www.pinehurst.com/faqs/))
- Nos. 2, 4, 6–11 are for resort guests or members only. Guests get priority tee times. The Cradle has year-round walk-on play.
- The Cradle is 9 holes, 789 yards, par 27. Resort rate is about under a hundred; replays are free.
- Extra rounds on Nos. 1/3/5 cost about under a hundred for resort guests ([GOLF.com](https://golf.com/travel/pinehurst-greens-fees-price-every-course/), may be dated).
- No. 2: resort guests only, 2-night minimum, +several hundred package premium. An extra second round is about several hundred peak / several hundred off-season ([GolfForKings](https://golfforkings.com/guides/pinehurst-no-2-green-fees/), third-party).
- No. 2 and No. 4 are cart-path only. No. 10 is walking only.
- Caddie under a hundred per bag (two players often share one). Forecaddie under a hundred per player. Single bag a couple of hundred. Suggested tips: under a hundred or more per bag for a caddie, under a hundred or more for a forecaddie.
- Club rental is under a hundred a day.

## 5. Kids
- Kids 17 and under stay free with a parent. **Two kids per paying adult play free** on Nos. 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and The Cradle. **Nos. 2, 4 and 10 charge an extra per-child fee (amount not published).**
- Kids 5 and under dine free; ages 6–17 pay à la carte ([FAQ](https://www.pinehurst.com/faqs/), [family page](https://www.pinehurst.com/itineraries/your-pinehurst-family-vacation/)).
- The Cradle: kids play free with a paying adult; ages 5 to 84 have made holes-in-one there ([news](https://www.pinehurst.com/news/10-of-the-best-things-you-can-do-in-pinehurst/)).
- Thistle Dhu: 18-hole putting course next to The Cradle. Free for resort guests; ask the starter for putters ([page](https://www.pinehurst.com/golf/courses/thistle-dhu-putting-course/)).
- Kids' Course: no current pinehurst.com detail was found. Its fee and location are **UNVERIFIED** (there's a "Kids' Course" reference only in user notes). Ask the resort.
- Main courses: kids are allowed and free on the courses listed above. On No. 2, 18 holes walking with a caddie is long for 10-year-olds. Junior tee options are not verified.

Published prices in this copy are ranges in words. Exact figures were removed before filing.

Built Thu Oct 15, 2026, 6:45 PM ET. Research only: nothing booked, nobody contacted.

## 1. Pinehurst Resort, NC (drive)
- **Getting there:** drive about 75 miles. No flight, so clubs travel free in the car.
- **Best lodging:** The Carolina Hotel (Pinehurst's flagship, 4-star tier) or Holly Inn [UNV for star rating]. There are no 5-star options nearby.
- **Golf for 3 with a cart rider:** **The Cradle** is a 9-hole short course (789 yards, kids welcome). **Thistle Dhu** is the putting course. **Pinehurst No. 2** and No. 4 are for Naresh. The boys could also play No. 1 or No. 3 (shorter) from forward tees. The resort runs junior clinics and the Pinehurst Golf Academy. **Note:** confirm the resort's minimum ages and its cart/rider rules for No. 2, which traditionally walks with caddies [UNV].
- **Park:** none nearby. This is golf only, with Weymouth Woods as a nature walk.
- **Best months:** Apr–May and late Sep–Nov.
- **Cost [EST]:** several thousand to around ten thousand for 5 nights, mostly lodging plus a round on No. 2.
- **Why #1:** no flight, the best kid golf infrastructure on this list, and an easy long weekend.

## 2. Scottsdale + Sedona + Grand Canyon (PHX)
- **Flights [V]:** RDU's list shows **American and Southwest**. Southwest says it flies **once daily, every day** ([southwest.com](https://www.southwest.com/en/flights/flights-from-raleigh-durham-to-phoenix)). FlightsFrom counts about 13 nonstops a week across both airlines ([flightsfrom.com/RDU-PHX](https://www.flightsfrom.com/RDU-PHX)). **Year-round.** Southwest's free checked bags help with the clubs.
- **Best lodging:** **The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort** (Marriott, 5-star, kid pools). Alternatives are Fairmont Scottsdale Princess or Four Seasons Troon North. In Sedona, **L'Auberge de Sedona** or **Enchantment Resort**. At the Grand Canyon, El Tovar (about 3-star, historic) or a day trip.
- **Golf:** The Phoenician course. **TPC Scottsdale.** **Troon North.** For kid-friendly options, Grayhawk has a learning center, and **Papago or the Phoenician's short-game areas** work for the boys [UNV for specific junior programs]. Arizona courses generally allow cart riders.
- **Park for 10-year-olds:** the **Grand Canyon South Rim** (about 3.5 hrs from Scottsdale): Junior Ranger badge, Rim Trail, Bright Angel first mile, sunset at Hopi Point. In Sedona, **Slide Rock State Park**, a Pink Jeep tour, and Bell Rock.
- **Best months:** Mar–Apr and Oct–Nov (Jun–Aug is extremely hot in Scottsdale; winter can mean snow at the canyon rim).
- **Cost [EST]:** around ten thousand to the low teens of thousands. Flights a couple of thousand to a few thousand, lodging a few thousand to several thousand, golf a couple of thousand to a few thousand, SUV around a thousand.

## 3. Kiawah Island Golf Resort, SC (drive)
- **Getting there:** drive about 4.5 hrs. **There is no RDU–CHS nonstop:** Charleston isn't on RDU's list [V], so driving is the way.
- **Best lodging:** **The Sanctuary at Kiawah Island** (Forbes 5-star).
- **Golf:** **Ocean Course** for Naresh (very hard; check the resort's junior age policy [UNV]). Turtle Point and Cougar Point for the family. The resort runs junior golf camps and a **Kiawah Golf Academy** [UNV for exact 2027 dates]. Carts are allowed on most courses.
- **Park:** no national park. Instead, beach, Night Heron Park nature center, dolphin tours, and Fort Sumter National Monument in Charleston.
- **Best months:** Apr–May and Sep–Oct.
- **Cost [EST]:** around ten thousand to the low teens of thousands. The Sanctuary is around a thousand to a couple of thousand or more a night in season.

## 4. Southern Utah via Las Vegas (Zion, Bryce)
- **Flights [V]:** RDU's list shows **Delta and Southwest**. Google Flights shows **Delta 7x a week (daily)**. Southwest's own page says daily, but one Google Flights snapshot showed Southwest on Sundays only, so **Southwest frequency is mixed or [UNV]** ([Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-raleigh-to-las-vegas.html), [southwest.com](https://www.southwest.com/en/flights/flights-from-raleigh-durham-to-las-vegas)). Year-round.
- **Best lodging:** In Vegas, **Waldorf Astoria** or **Four Seasons** (no casino, family-friendly; Marriott option: Vdara/Cosmopolitan). Near Zion, **Amangiri** (about 2 hrs further, ultra-luxury) or **Inn at Entrada / Black Desert Resort (St. George)**, which is 4–5-star [UNV for rating].
- **Golf:** **Shadow Creek** (expensive, guest-access rules), and Wolf Creek in Mesquite. In St. George, **Black Desert** (new) and **Sand Hollow**, with par-3s for the boys [UNV].
- **Park for 10-year-olds:** **Zion** Riverside Walk, the lower Narrows in summer, Pa'rus Trail bikes, and the Junior Ranger program. **Bryce** Navajo Loop among the hoodoos. Valley of Fire on the drive.
- **Best months:** Apr–May and late Sep–Oct.
- **Cost [EST]:** around ten thousand to the low teens of thousands.

## 5. Sea Island, GA (drive or JAX)
- **Flights [V]:** **Breeze RDU–JAX, Tue/Thu/Sun (3x a week), year-round**, A220, about 1.5 hrs ([flybreeze.com](https://www.flybreeze.com/shopping/en-us/flights-from-raleigh-nc-to-jacksonville-fl), [KAYAK](https://www.kayak.com/flight-routes/Raleigh-Durham-RDU/Jacksonville-JAX)). It's about 75 minutes from JAX to Sea Island. **There is no RDU–SAV nonstop:** Savannah isn't on RDU's list [V]. Driving takes about 6 hrs.
- **Best lodging:** **The Cloister at Sea Island** or **The Lodge** (both Forbes 5-star).
- **Golf:** Seaside, Plantation and Retreat courses. The **Golf Performance Center** offers junior lessons and family clinics [UNV for the 2027 program]. Carts are allowed.
- **Park:** no national park. Cumberland Island National Seashore (wild horses, by ferry) is the best nearby nature day.
- **Best months:** Apr–May and Oct–Nov.
- **Cost [EST]:** around ten thousand to the low teens of thousands.

## 6. Pebble Beach / Monterey (SFO)
- **Flights [V]:** **United only**, year-round, about **2–5 a day** (FlightQ 2–3 a day; 2LNR about 29 a week) ([2lnr.com](https://2lnr.com/routes/rdu-sfo), [flightq.app](https://flightq.app/from/rdu/to/united-states)). This fits Naresh's United Premier Platinum. SFO to Monterey is about 2 hrs by car.
- **Best lodging:** **The Lodge at Pebble Beach** or **The Inn at Spanish Bay** (both 5-star tier).
- **Golf:** Pebble Beach Golf Links (around a thousand or more per player [EST]). **Peter Hay Golf Course**, a 9-hole par-3 at Pebble, **is free for kids 12 and under with a paying adult** [UNV: confirm 2027 policy]. Spanish Bay. Pacific Grove muni.
- **Park:** **Pinnacles NP** (talus caves, about 1.25 hrs) and **Monterey Bay Aquarium**. Point Lobos. Yosemite is about 4 hrs away, so make it a separate leg.
- **Best months:** Sep–Oct (warmest and clearest). Summer is foggy.
- **Cost [EST]:** the low teens of thousands to the high teens of thousands (Pebble lodging and green fees drive the cost).

## 7. Rocky Mountain NP via Denver
- **Flights [V]:** **United, Southwest and Frontier**, about **4–5 a day**, year-round ([FlightQ](https://flightq.app/from/rdu/to/united-states), RDU list).
- **Best lodging:** **The Broadmoor** in Colorado Springs (Forbes 5-star, golf), paired with Estes Park (**The Stanley**, about 4-star [UNV]) or **Four Seasons Vail / Park Hyatt Beaver Creek** (Hyatt). The family is already going to Beaver Creek in Mar 2027.
- **Golf:** The Broadmoor's East and West courses plus a short course [UNV]. Junior lessons at the Broadmoor Golf Academy. Sanctuary is private.
- **Park for 10-year-olds:** **Rocky Mountain NP**: Bear Lake, Alberta Falls, Trail Ridge Road (open roughly late May to Oct), elk, and Junior Ranger. Timed-entry permits are required in summer.
- **Best months:** Jun–Sep.
- **Cost [EST]:** around ten thousand to the low teens of thousands.

## 8. Utah via Salt Lake City
- **Flights [V]:** **Delta only**, **daily** at about 7:00 AM (DL580/DL967), year-round ([FlightMapper](https://info.flightmapper.net/route/Delta_Air_Lines_DL_RDU_SLC), [KAYAK](https://www.kayak.com/flight-routes/Raleigh-Durham-RDU/Salt-Lake-City-SLC)).
- **Best lodging:** **Montage Deer Valley** or **St. Regis Deer Valley** (Marriott, 5-star) in Park City. In Moab, **Hoodoo Moab (Hilton Curio)** or **Sorrel River Ranch** [UNV for rating].
- **Golf:** Park City courses (Promontory is private, so check access [UNV]) and Soldier Hollow.
- **Park:** **Arches** (Delicate Arch, Sand Dune Arch, timed entry) and **Canyonlands Island in the Sky**, about 4 hrs from SLC.
- **Best months:** Jun–Sep (Park City), Apr–May and Sep–Oct (Moab).
- **Cost [EST]:** around ten thousand to the low teens of thousands.

## 9. Puerto Rico: Dorado + El Yunque (SJU)
- **Flights [V]:** **JetBlue** daily or near-daily (Google Flights snapshot: 5x a week, Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri/Sat/Sun), **Frontier** Thu/Sun from Nov 22, 2026, and **Delta from Dec 19, 2026** ([Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-raleigh-to-san-juan.html), [flightsfrom.com/RDU-SJU](https://www.flightsfrom.com/RDU-SJU)). Year-round.
- **Best lodging:** **Dorado Beach, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve** (where the family is staying now) or St. Regis Bahia Beach.
- **Golf:** Dorado's East course (TPC) and Bahia Beach. Carts are standard.
- **Park:** **El Yunque National Forest** (the only tropical rainforest in the US forest system): La Mina Falls and Yokahú Tower. Plus the bioluminescent bay.
- **Best months:** Dec–Apr.
- **Cost [EST]:** around ten thousand to the low teens of thousands. This ranks lower only because the family is there now. Use the debrief to decide whether to repeat it.

## 10. Cancun / Riviera Maya (CUN)
- **Flights [V]:** **American, Delta, Aeromexico and Frontier** are on RDU's list. **Frequency and seasonality are [UNV]:** I couldn't find a 2026–27 frequency source. Check Google Flights before deciding.
- **Best lodging:** **Rosewood Mayakoba**, **Fairmont Mayakoba**, or **Banyan Tree** (5-star). **Hyatt:** Andaz Mayakoba. The **El Camaleón** golf course is on site.
- **Golf:** El Camaleón (former PGA Tour) and Iberostar Playa Paraíso. Kids' programs [UNV].
- **Park:** no US national park, but there are cenotes, Xcaret / Xel-Há, and the Tulum ruins. Counts as a "new country" only if Mexico is new to the family (they have a Mexico City idea on their wishlist).
- **Best months:** Dec–Apr (Jun–Nov is hurricane season).
- **Cost [EST]:** around ten thousand to the low teens of thousands. Needs a passport.

Visit Lake Louise, Moraine Lake by Parks Canada shuttle, and Johnston Canyon. Best months: Jul–Sep. Cost [EST] the low teens of thousands to the high teens of thousands.

# Puerto Rico: Dorado and El Yunque

Drawn from the Oct 15, 2026 RDU nonstop shortlist. Research only. Nothing booked. Published prices are omitted.

**Travelers:** Naresh (golfs), Renu (rides in the cart), and the two boys. **Maps list:** San Juan + Dorado Eats (Sylvia). URL not on file.

## Why it is on the list
RDU–SJU is a real nonstop, and the trip pairs a resort golf course with El Yunque. It ranks toward the bottom of the top 10 only because the family is there now. Use the debrief before planning a repeat.

## Stay and golf
- **Dorado Beach, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve** is the stay the family is using now. St. Regis Bahia Beach is the alternate.
- Golf: Dorado’s East course (TPC) and Bahia Beach. Carts are standard.

## Park
**El Yunque National Forest** (the only tropical rainforest in the US forest system): La Mina Falls and Yokahú Tower, plus a bioluminescent bay.

## When
December through April.

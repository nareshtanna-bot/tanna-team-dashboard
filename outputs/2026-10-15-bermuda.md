Published prices in this copy are ranges in words. Exact figures were removed before filing.
Travelers: Naresh (golfs), Renu, Nishanth (10) and Shaan (~9–10), both taking golf lessons. Flying from RDU, bringing clubs as checked bags. Hotels: 5-star preferred, 4-star minimum.
Researched Oct 15, 2026. This builds on `bermuda-jul2027-rick.md`. **Nothing has been booked or contacted.**
Labels: [F] = fact from a cited source. [E] = estimate. **[UNVERIFIED]** = could not confirm.

## 1. Recommended pick
**Rosewood Bermuda (Tucker's Point), 6 nights, in late May–June or late Sept–Oct.** Rosewood is the only 5-star option open right now that has its own championship course, a private beach club, and a kids' club all on one property. Mid Ocean is right next door. Keep **Hamilton Princess** as the backup if the price is lower or the dates suit it better. **Fairmont Southampton** is the one to watch once it reopens in 2027.

## 4. Day by day (6 nights at Rosewood. For 5 nights, drop Day 5)

## 7. When to go
- **Best: mid-May through June**, then **late Sept–Oct** as the second choice. The water is warm, the beach clubs are open (Rosewood's runs Mar–Oct), and both are outside July–Aug peak pricing.
- **Hurricanes:** the season runs Jun 1–Nov 30 and peaks Sept–Oct [F, gotobermuda]. Buy travel insurance if going in the fall.
- **Too cool for the beach: about Nov/Dec–April.** Water is in the high 60s°F and air in the low 70s [E/UNVERIFIED exact figures]. Good for golf but not for swimming. July–Aug: about 85°F, sea 81–84°F, most crowded and most expensive [F].
